Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy applies to the mobile games Arrow Puzzle and Wheel of Words published by DJCI Games.

What we collect

We do not require you to create an account or enter your name, email, or payment details.

The games show ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect a device advertising ID and approximate technical data (device type, OS version, general location derived from IP) to show and measure ads.

Game progress (level, settings, language, daily challenge status) is stored only on your device.

How data is used

  • To show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads
  • To measure ad performance
  • To save your local game progress

Third parties

Advertising is provided by Google AdMob. Google’s policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

These games are not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID in the device settings. Uninstalling the app removes local progress stored on the device.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on our GitHub profile or contact the developer account listed on the store page.
